# PDF CMap Resources

`SolidPDFGraphics` includes the predefined Adobe CMaps required by PDF simple and composite font interpretation. The files under `Resources/AdobeCMaps` were generated by Adobe and imported without semantic modification from [`adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources`](https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources) at commit `f5cf3bca7fdfeaceb77aa82847e974f2306c20b4`.

The PDF character-collection mappings under `Resources/AdobePDFMappings` were imported without semantic modification from [`adobe-type-tools/mapping-resources-pdf`](https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/mapping-resources-pdf) at commit `2dd5e53fb74a01718b9dfd448a0d1cce6fff2aa5`.

Both upstream licenses permit redistribution. Complete license texts are checked in beside the resources. Builds consume only these pinned files and never fetch or regenerate mapping data.
